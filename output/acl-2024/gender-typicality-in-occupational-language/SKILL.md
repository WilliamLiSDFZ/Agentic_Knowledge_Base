# Gender Typicality In Occupational Language

This research area examines how occupational titles carry implicit gender associations in language, using corpus linguistics methods to measure the statistical distributions of gender-marked language (pronouns, modifiers, co-occurring terms) around occupational nouns. The work quantifies gender typicality scores for occupations and investigates how linguistic context encodes and reinforces social stereotypes, with applications to bias detection
