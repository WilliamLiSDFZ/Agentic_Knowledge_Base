# BiasDPO: Mitigating Bias in Language Models through Direct Preference Optimization

**Source**: https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-srw.7/

## [POSITIVE] Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)
A loss function-based training approach that maximizes log probability of preferred (less biased) completions and minimizes log probability of dispreferred (biased) completions, without requiring a separate reward model

**Delta**: +0.15 BBQ accuracy (0.5 to 0.65)
**Condition**: Applied to Microsoft Phi-2 model for bias mitigation across gender, race, and religion categories

**Evidence**: "The results show that the IPO variation of the DPO loss function with a β value of 0.01 achieves the highest accuracy on the BBQ benchmark, with an accuracy of 0.65, compared to the baseline accuracy of 0.5."

## [POSITIVE] Identity Preference Optimization (IPO)
A variation of DPO that adds a regularization term controlling the gap between log-likelihood ratios of preferred and dispreferred completions to prevent overfitting

**Delta**: Best performing variant at 0.65 BBQ accuracy vs 0.5 baseline
**Condition**: Best results achieved with β=0.01; outperforms standard DPO, SLiC, and KTO variants on BBQ benchmark

**Evidence**: "The results show that the IPO variation of the DPO loss function with a β value of 0.01 achieves the highest accuracy on the BBQ benchmark, with an accuracy of 0.65, compared to the baseline accuracy of 0.5."

## [POSITIVE] Sequence Likelihood Calibration (SLiC)
A DPO variation that adds a rank calibration term and cross-entropy loss term to reduce overfitting

**Delta**: Outperforms baseline but underperforms IPO on BBQ
**Condition**: Applied to Phi-2 for bias reduction; compared against DPO, IPO, and KTO variants

**Evidence**: "Sequence Likelihood Calibration (SLiC) is another variation that adds a rank calibration term and cross-entropy loss term to the loss function, which has been shown to reduce overfitting as well."

## [POSITIVE] Kahneman-Tversky Optimization (KTO)
A DPO variant that maximizes utility of generations using prospect theory, requiring only a binary desirable/undesirable signal rather than paired preferences

**Delta**: Outperforms baseline but underperforms IPO on BBQ
**Condition**: Applied to Phi-2 for bias reduction; more practical for real-world applications due to binary signal requirement

**Evidence**: "Kahneman-Tversky Optimization (KTO) is another variation that directly maximizes the utility of generations using a model of human utility based on Kahneman & Tversky's prospect theory."

## [POSITIVE] Low β hyperparameter value
Setting the β hyperparameter (which controls divergence from reference model) to lower values such as 0.01

**Delta**: Best accuracy at β=0.01 across all DPO variants
**Condition**: Applies across all DPO loss function variants tested on BBQ benchmark

**Evidence**: "In general, results show that lower values of β tend to perform better than higher values in reducing bias in the language generated by the model."

## [POSITIVE] Manually curated BiasDPO dataset
A dataset of 1,100+ entries with prompts paired with biased and unbiased completions covering gender, racial, and religious biases, manually crafted and augmented with GPT-4 generated samples

**Delta**: Enabled model to outperform baseline on all benchmarks
**Condition**: Used as training data for DPO-based bias mitigation on Phi-2

**Evidence**: "The dataset contains more than 1,100 entries covering biases related to gender, race, and religion, as well as their intersections, across a wide range of topics and contexts."

## [POSITIVE] GPT-4 dataset augmentation
Using GPT-4 to generate additional training samples beyond the manually crafted ones, with careful review and refinement

**Delta**: Expanded dataset to 1,100+ entries
**Condition**: Applied to expand the BiasDPO training dataset

**Evidence**: "To expand the dataset, we used GPT-4 to generate additional samples. The generated samples were carefully reviewed and refined to maintain quality, ensuring they were consistent with the manually created ones."

## [POSITIVE] KL divergence regularization via reference model
Using a frozen reference model in the DPO loss to prevent the fine-tuned model from deviating too far from the original data distribution

**Delta**: Prevents model degradation while reducing bias
**Condition**: Applied in all DPO variants during Phi-2 fine-tuning

**Evidence**: "The reference model is used in order to prevent the model from deviating too much from the original distribution of the data using the Kullback–Leibler divergence term."

## [POSITIVE] BiasDPO on RealToxicityPrompts
Applying the BiasDPO framework to reduce toxic content generation

**Delta**: 0.11 toxicity score vs 0.17 baseline and 0.14 for Mistral-7B
**Condition**: Phi-2 + BiasDPO evaluated on RealToxicityPrompts benchmark

**Evidence**: "Our model achieves the lowest toxicity score on the RealToxicityPrompts benchmark, as it has a score of 0.11, compared to the baseline score of 0.17, and 0.14 for Mistral-7B, the second lowest score."

## [POSITIVE] BiasDPO on TruthfulQA
Applying the BiasDPO framework evaluated on truthfulness benchmark

**Delta**: +0.03 accuracy (0.42 to 0.45)
**Condition**: Phi-2 + BiasDPO evaluated on TruthfulQA benchmark

**Evidence**: "Our model also achieves the highest accuracy on the TruthfulQA benchmark, with a score of 0.45, compared to the baseline score of 0.42."

## [NEGATIVE] BiasDPO on BOLD race and religion categories
Applying BiasDPO evaluated on BOLD benchmark race and religion bias categories

**Delta**: Race: 0.0164 vs baseline 0.0144; Religion: 0.0613 vs baseline 0.0469
**Condition**: Phi-2 + BiasDPO evaluated on BOLD benchmark race and religion subcategories specifically

**Evidence**: "On the BOLD benchmark, our model achieves the lowest score on the gender bias category, while being less performant on race and religion categories compared to Gemma-2B that achieves the best scores."

## [POSITIVE] BiasDPO on BBQ gender and race categories
Applying BiasDPO evaluated on BBQ benchmark gender and race subcategories

**Delta**: Gender: 0.68 vs 0.60 baseline; Race: 0.87 vs 0.77 baseline
**Condition**: Phi-2 + BiasDPO evaluated on BBQ gender and race subcategories

**Evidence**: "On the BBQ benchmark, our model achieves the highest accuracy on the gender, and race categories by a large margin, with scores of 0.68 and 0.87, respectively, compared to the baseline scores of 0.6 and 0.77."

## [NEUTRAL] Small model size (2.7B parameters)
Applying BiasDPO to a relatively small 2.7B parameter model (Phi-2) rather than larger state-of-the-art models

**Delta**: Outperforms similarly-sized models but not always larger ones like Mistral-7B
**Condition**: Limitation noted for generalizability; Mistral-7B (7B params) outperforms on BBQ religion and BOLD overall

**Evidence**: "In this study, we tested our approach on the Phi-2 model, which is a 2.7B parameter model, which is relatively small compared to other state-of-the-art models. Future work should focus on testing this approach on larger models."
